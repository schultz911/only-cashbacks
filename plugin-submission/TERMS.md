Draft for Kiran Saldanha's review and legal approval; not a published agreement. Any applicable liability, governing-law, dispute, eligibility and other legal clauses must be decided by the operator before publication rather than assumed by this draft.

## Service

OnlyCashbacks India compares Indian retailer listings and calculates card-benefit candidates using the selected cards and settings of the user's own signed-in OnlyCashbacks account. The app's displayed card selection is treated as the user's owned cards. Users manage that selection and their saved usage/settings in the OnlyCashbacks app.

## User account and access

Use an account you are authorized to access. Do not provide another person's credentials, attempt to retrieve another account's saved data, or place passwords, OTPs, tokens or private keys in chat. Each installer signs in separately. The plugin does not provide an administrative view of other users' accounts.

## Comparisons and transactions

Prices, availability, cashback eligibility, issuer rules and retailer offers may change. Comparisons depend on current listing evidence and the user's saved settings; verify the retailer's checkout and issuer terms before deciding to purchase. The plugin does not place orders, execute payments, apply for cards or edit saved wallet data.
