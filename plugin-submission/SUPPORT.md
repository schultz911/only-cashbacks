# OnlyCashbacks India support

Draft for creator approval and publication.

For help with OnlyCashbacks India, account connection, card selection or saved settings, email **schultz911@gmail.com**.

For account-specific help, contact support from the email associated with the OnlyCashbacks account. Describe what you expected, the observed error and when it occurred. Do not send passwords, OTPs, Firebase ID tokens, private keys, card numbers or other credentials.

To request deletion of saved wallet data, email the same address and state that you want your OnlyCashbacks saved data deleted. Account ownership must be verified before deletion. This support page does not provide an automated deletion feature or promise a response/deletion deadline.
